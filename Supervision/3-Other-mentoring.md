# Other Mentoring Roles

### University teaching

I teach in the program: *Artificial Intelligence in Medicine* at Sorbonne Université. This program is a set of lectures and hands-on tutorial for second and third year university students in medicine (DFGSM2 & DFGSM3) that chose the *Artificial Intelligence* speciality. I teach (1) an introduction to programming and scientific analysis in *python*, (2) an introduction to machine learning and data science and (3) a lecture on the relationship between neuroscience and artificial intelligence.

My teaching material is available at: https://yzerlaut.github.io/teaching
